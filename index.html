About

I'm a Test Architect at Nokia, leading end-to-end validation of anyRAN and AI-RAN deployments across labs, customer sites, and field trials. Over the past five years I've shipped Cloud RAN PoCs, hardened Kubernetes-based CaaS platforms, automated CI/CD pipelines, and presented next-generation telecom solutions to global customers and R&D leadership.

My career started in cybersecurity at Trend Micro and Lion Re:Sources, where I learned to think defensively. That mindset still shapes how I approach everything from container scanning with Trivy to compliance validation in production-bound RAN software. I hold an M.S. in Cybersecurity (4.0 GPA), a B.S. in Computer Science (Cum Laude), and certifications across AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud.

I love mentoring, and I've led Nokia's intern cohorts every summer since 2023. The work I find most energizing is the messy, cross-discipline kind that shows up when AI, cloud, and 5G all meet in the same lab.
